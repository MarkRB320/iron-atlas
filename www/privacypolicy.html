Iron Atlas and Health Connect

You can choose to send the body weight you enter in Iron Atlas to Health Connect. When you switch this on and grant permission, Iron Atlas writes your new weight entries and their dates to Health Connect on this phone.

Iron Atlas requests permission to write weight. It does not read your Health Connect records. Other apps can access the entries only through the permissions you give them in Health Connect.

Turn off weight sharing in Iron Atlas account settings to stop future writes. You can revoke the permission or delete previously shared weights in Health Connect. Your Iron Atlas measurement log is managed separately in the app.
