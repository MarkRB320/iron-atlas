Using the app

You must be aged 16 or over to use Iron Atlas.

Iron Atlas provides exercise demonstrations, workout planning and record keeping for general fitness. Its suggestions use information such as your selected goal, equipment, experience and recorded activity. Choose exercises and loads suited to your circumstances, and seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns or your suitability for exercise.

You can use guest features on your device. Account backup, synchronisation and your personal trainer connection need an Iron Atlas account, an internet connection and your separate choice to enable cloud backup. Signing in also allows you to keep training records on your device. Features that use device permissions depend on a compatible device and the permission you choose to grant.

Keep your account credentials secure. Use the app for your own records or for clients who choose to connect with you. Respect other people's privacy and rights when entering or sharing content, and use only content you are entitled to provide.

Your content and trainer connections

Your rights in your own photos, notes and other original content remain yours. When you ask the app to back up, display or share that content, you allow the processing and transmission needed to provide that feature.

Connecting to a trainer lets them see the training summary described in the app and send plans to you. Any separate coaching arrangement should be agreed directly with the trainer. Ask the trainer about the plans and advice they provide.

Trainers must use connected clients' information for the coaching purpose agreed with those clients, keep it secure and respect requests relating to copies they hold. Clients can disconnect using the trainer section in Plans.

The privacy notice explains the information used by the app and the sharing choices available.

Safe sharing and reporting

Shared plans, names and notes must respect other users. Unlawful, abusive, hateful, threatening, sexually explicit or exploitative content and spam are prohibited. Shared text is screened before publication. If your text is rejected, review it before trying again.

Use the app's report and block controls for shared content or an unwanted connection, or contact mark@insomnia-it.co.uk. We review reports promptly and may remove content or restrict an abusive account's publishing and connection features. You can contact support to ask for a decision to be reviewed.

Blocking ends the affected connection and prevents further shared delivery between those accounts. Unblocking removes your block. Reconnecting with a trainer requires fresh connection consent. Copies already saved or exported remain under the controls of their destination.

Records and synchronisation

The app keeps records locally and, when you enable cloud backup, synchronises account records. A device can have changes waiting to upload while it is offline. Where the app identifies incompatible changes, it asks you to review the copies available before choosing how to continue.

Contact support if records appear to be missing or a synchronisation problem continues. Sharing a plan code or exporting a file gives the recipient access to the content included in that share.

Disconnecting or deleting your account

You can turn off cloud backup in Account while retaining your sign-in and a device copy. This stops uploads and removes your personal trainer connection, shared summary, waiting plans sent to you and plan codes you published. The app preserves the device copy before completing removal of the cloud photos and training records. If interrupted, return to Account to finish removal. Your trainer profile and work with other clients remain available if you use Coach.

You can disconnect from a trainer in Plans. You can also delete your Iron Atlas account in the app or through account tools. Review the deletion explanation and confirm the account you want to remove.

To stop future subscription charges, cancel the subscription separately in the Apple or Google account that made the purchase. Manage copies already exported or stored on another device through the relevant destination or device.
